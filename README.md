# 🏆 Competition Overview

In 2025, our team traveled to **Dubai** to compete in a **FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC)** event.

After preparing together as a team, we participated in the competition with our robot and competed against other teams in a challenging FTC environment.

Our team successfully reached the **Finalist Alliance** and finished the competition in **2nd place**.

We also received the **Control Award** for our robot's control system.

This event was an important milestone for our team and gave us valuable experience in competitive robotics, engineering, programming, robot operation, teamwork, and match strategy.
